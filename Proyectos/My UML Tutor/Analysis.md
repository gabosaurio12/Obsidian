## User stories

> The user logs in and can immediately start to practice.

> The system shows a card with the UML symbol (it can be a class, a relation, etc.), and three answer options, one is correct, another one is wrong but it's something similar in style or meaning and the last one is completely wrong.

> After eight questions you get the results, something like "7/8".

> The user can play again with a totally diferent orther of questions

## Functionalities

- The questions must be dynamic so they don't repeat.
- The system must have a streak system that activates if you practice at least one time everyday.

## Touch and feel

- The style of the app is "hand made drawing"
